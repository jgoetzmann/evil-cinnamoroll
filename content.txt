#     VS Code). Emoji are text too, so you can change those as well.

#   * To see your edits on your own computer, open the site through a web

#                        The 👁️ button hides/shows these.
#   {drop}, {last_price} ...  numbers the page fills in itself. Keep them
#                        exactly as they are; move them around if you like.
